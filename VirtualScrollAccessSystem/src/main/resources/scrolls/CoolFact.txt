Did you know that octopuses have three hearts? 
Two pump blood through the gills, while the third pumps it to 
the rest of the body. Even more fascinating is that they can 
change the color and texture of their skin to blend in with their 
surroundings, making them the ultimate underwater chameleons. 
These intelligent creatures can even solve puzzles and use tools, 
making them one of the most intriguing animals in the ocean.
