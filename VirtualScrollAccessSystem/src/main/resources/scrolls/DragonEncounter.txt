The dragon's breath illuminated the night sky, casting long shadows across the battlefield. Warriors, clad in armor, stood motionless, their swords raised in a futile attempt to shield themselves from the inevitable. But one figure, cloaked in silver and wielding a staff of pure light, stepped forward. The ground trembled beneath her feet, but her resolve was unwavering. With a whisper, she summoned the ancient power of the land itself, drawing strength from the earth, wind, and stars. The dragon, sensing its end, let out a final roar that echoed through the ages.
