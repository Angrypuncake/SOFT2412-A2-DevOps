On a rainy afternoon, Sarah sat by the window with an old, yellowed letter in her hands. It had arrived unexpectedly, decades after it was written. The handwriting was familiar, yet distant—her grandmother's. The letter spoke of love, loss, and choices never revealed to Sarah until now. As she read the final line, her heart raced. Her grandmother had left behind a secret, one that would change everything Sarah thought she knew about her family. The answers lay hidden in a house no one had lived in for fifty years.
